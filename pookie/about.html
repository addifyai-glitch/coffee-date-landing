Meet people. Create moments. Let chemistry do the rest.

Most apps ask you to fall in love with a profile. We think that's backwards. A grid of photos and a witty bio can't tell you how someone laughs, whether a conversation flows, or if a Tuesday coffee turns into three hours you didn't plan for. Chemistry can't be predicted — it can only be experienced. So we built Pookie around getting people into the same room sooner, and out of endless messaging threads that go nowhere.

Meet first, chat less

Pookie is a modern social discovery platform designed around one simple belief: the fastest way to know if you connect with someone is to actually spend time with them. Instead of optimizing for message counts, we optimize for real plans — a walk, a coffee, a shared activity — so that "getting to know someone" happens in person, not in a scroll.

Vibe Mode, not a permanent label

People aren't static, and we don't think an app should force you into one. Vibe Mode lets you signal what kind of energy you're open to right now — casual, adventurous, low-key, ready to make plans — without locking you into a fixed identity or profile category forever. It shifts as you do.

Privacy-first, by design

You control what you share, and with whom. We build with the assumption that your data belongs to you, not to us. That shapes everything from how we store information to how visibility works inside the product — details before they become features, never the other way around.

AI that assists, never decides

We're building AI into Pookie to help with the busywork — surfacing shared interests, reducing awkward small talk, helping plans come together — but it will never choose who you talk to or make the call for you. Human choice stays at the center. Assistance, not automation of connection.

Where we are today

Right now, Pookie is in early access — we don't have a public product yet. We're validating this idea with real people before we build the full experience: talking to potential users, collecting honest feedback, and using a waitlist to understand who's interested and why. Trust and thoughtful design come before shipping features, so we'd rather take the time to build something worth trusting than rush something forgettable.

If any of this resonates, we'd love to have you along for the early part of the journey.
