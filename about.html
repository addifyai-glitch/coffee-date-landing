Meet for coffee. Actually meet.

Most apps ask you to fall in love with a profile, then keep you talking for weeks before anything real happens. We think that's backwards. A bio can't tell you how someone laughs, whether a conversation flows, or if a Tuesday coffee turns into three hours you didn't plan for. So Pookie skips the profile-scrolling entirely and gets straight to the part that matters: an actual invite, to an actual place, at an actual time.

An invite, not a match

Send someone a coffee invite — a place, a date, a time, and a note if you want one. They get a single email with three honest options: accept, suggest another time, or decline. No swiping, no app to install, no back-and-forth just to land on a time that works.

Real plans beat endless chat

Once an invite is accepted, both people get a calendar invite by email. That's it — no messaging thread to maintain, no algorithm deciding what you see next. Just a plan, on the calendar, ready to happen.

Privacy by design

The site never talks to the database directly — every signup and invite goes through a server that validates it, rate-limits it, and stores it behind a database with no public read access at all. Your details exist to make the invite or the waitlist work, and nothing else.

Where we are today

Pookie is in early access. The invite flow works today — try it above. Everything past that (accounts, discovery, the rest of the product) is being built based on who joins the waitlist and what they tell us they need.
