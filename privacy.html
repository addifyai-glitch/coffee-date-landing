Privacy policy

Pookie is currently in early access. We're validating this idea before building the full product, which means the amount of data we collect is intentionally small, and mostly optional. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, and how it's protected — no legal boilerplate, just the truth about how the site actually works. For our formal GDPR/DSGVO disclosures (relevant if you're in the EU/EEA), see Datenschutz and Impressum.

What we collect

Depending on which form you use, we may collect:

  • Waitlist sign-up: name and email (email required to confirm your spot), and city — all optional except email.
  • Invite feature: your name and email, the recipient's name and email, the place, date, time, and an optional message. Your email, their email, the place, and the date/time are required to send an invite; everything else is optional.

We do not require account creation, and we never ask for a password, payment details, or government ID. We also record a one-way hash of your IP address on form submissions, used only to prevent spam and abuse — never your raw IP address, and never linked to your identity.

Where your data lives

Every form on this site submits to our own server, which validates the request and writes it to a database with no public access at all — not for anonymous visitors, and not even for a signed-in one. The browser itself never talks to the database directly. Only we can view submitted data, through a private, authenticated tool that isn't publicly accessible.

Email notifications

When you use the waitlist or invite feature, our server sends the relevant emails (confirmation, invite delivery, response notifications, calendar confirmations) through Resend, a transactional email provider. Your email address is only used to deliver the message you're expecting — we don't use it for marketing or newsletters, and we don't sell or share it with advertisers.

How we use your information

We use what you share to: operate the invite feature, keep you informed if and when a fuller product launches, understand whether people actually want something like Pookie, and shape decisions about what to build next.

Your rights (including under GDPR)

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete any data you've submitted at any time, no questions asked. If you're in the EU/EEA, this includes the rights guaranteed under the GDPR — access, rectification, erasure, and data portability. Just email hello@addify.ae with the email or details you used, and we'll act on it.

This will evolve

Because Pookie is pre-launch and still being validated, this policy may change as the product — and the data we handle — grows. If we move from early access into a real, user-facing product, we'll update this page to reflect that, and any material changes will be clearly noted here.
