Frequently asked questions

What is Pookie? ⌄

Pookie is a simple way to turn "we should meet up" into an actual plan. Send someone a real invite — a place, a date, a time — and they get one email to accept, suggest another time, or decline. No swiping, no account required for them to respond.

Is this a dating app? ⌄

Not really — there are no profiles to browse and no matching. It's a lightweight invite tool: useful for a date, a catch-up with a friend, or a networking coffee. What it's "for" is up to you and the person you invite.

Does the person I invite need an account? ⌄

No. They just click accept, suggest another time, or decline from the email — nothing to sign up for.

What happens once an invite is accepted? ⌄

Both of you get an email confirming the details, with a calendar file (.ics) attached so it's one tap to add to your calendar.

Is my data safe? Who can see what I submit? ⌄

The site never talks to the database directly. Every signup and invite goes through a server that validates and stores it in a database with no public read access at all — not even to a logged-in visitor. Only we can read it, through a private, authenticated tool. See our privacy policy for details.

What's the waitlist for? ⌄

We're building past the invite flow into a fuller product, and the waitlist is how we decide what to build next and who hears about it first. Joining moves you up 5 spots for every friend you refer.

Do I need to pay anything right now? ⌄

No. The invite flow and the waitlist are both free during early access.

How do I get in touch? ⌄

Email us any time at irfan@addify.ae, or visit the contact page. We're a small early-access project, so replies may take a bit of time, but we read everything.

Can I delete my data? ⌄

Yes, any time and with no questions asked. Email irfan@addify.ae with the details you submitted (or the email address you used), and we'll remove them.
